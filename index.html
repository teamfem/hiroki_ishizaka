Two research programmes

Geometry and dynamics, connected by the need for reliable computation.

Programme I · Finite element analysis

Anisotropic finite elements

I study how mesh geometry controls approximation, stability, monotonicity and computable error certification.

Aim
Certified finite element analysis under computable geometric conditions, with quantitative bounds that keep geometric deterioration visible.

A central concern is accuracy on anisotropic meshes, where standard bounds can hide the effect of element shape inside unspecified constants.

Anisotropic FEM · Mesh geometry · CR / RT · Morley · Nitsche / DG · Stokes / Navier–Stokes · Equilibrated flux
FEM research overview →
Programme II · Evolution equations

Stable reduction of unresolved dynamics

I study what mathematical structure survives when latent or unresolved variables are eliminated from evolution equations.

Aim
Identify stable state spaces, sharp stability boundaries and certifiable approximation principles for reduced models with memory, delay or hidden internal states.

The long-term goal is reliable prediction and control when dynamically relevant states are only partially resolved.

Unresolved dynamics · Memory · Delay · Graph spaces · Coercivity · Structure preservation · Certified discretisation

A shared question

What remains stable, accurate and certifiable after information has been discretised, eliminated or compressed?

Across both programmes

AI4Math &
Mathematical Assurance

Mathematics for reliable scientific AI.

I am exploring mathematical foundations for reliable AI for Science. The aim is to quantify information lost through learning, model reduction and numerical discretisation, and to assess whether scientific predictions can be trusted.

Current directions connect certified PDE computation, structure-preserving learning of unresolved dynamics, memory and reduced models, and self-correcting scientific computation supported by computable error bounds.

AI4Math · Mathematical assurance · Certified scientific computing · Unresolved dynamics · Structure-preserving learning

About

I am an independent researcher in numerical analysis. My work spans finite element analysis, partial differential equations and mathematical certification.

I am particularly interested in mathematical structures that computation or model reduction can leave unseen: discretisation error, mesh geometry, unresolved scales, memory and latent states.
