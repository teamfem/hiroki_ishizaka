Research pipeline

What comes after the current manuscripts.

Candidate papers, theorems under investigation, flagship programmes and exact-curved geometry projects are shown separately from completed work.

A1 · Candidate paper

Discrete maximum principles for Crouzeix–Raviart finite elements on anisotropic simplicial meshes

Separates semi-regularity from metric-angle monotonicity and studies CR degrees of freedom, positivity-preserving loads and RT-based conservative reconstruction.

A2 · Candidate paper

Maximum-norm error estimates on anisotropic simplicial meshes under computable geometric and connectivity conditions

Separates directional interpolation error from patch connectivity and investigates discrete Green functions, Ritz stability and DMP.

A3 · Memory series

Structure-preserving fully discrete approximation of history-dependent diffusion with measure-valued memory

BDF and convolution quadrature interpreted through hidden internal variables, with discrete cross-term cancellation and graph-norm stability.

T1 · Theorem under investigation

Three-dimensional Nédélec interpolation under computable anisotropic geometry

Quantitative stability, weak boundary moments, curl commutativity and genuinely three-dimensional tetrahedral structure.

T2 · Theorem under investigation

Discrete maximum principle versus maximum-norm stability

Clarifies the distinction between order preservation and quantitative pointwise accuracy.

F1 · Flagship

Anisotropic de Rham stability on semi-regular tetrahedral meshes

Nédélec–RT stability, commuting projections, discrete Poincaré inequalities, Hodge decomposition and global grading/connectivity conditions.

F2 · Flagship

Certified Maxwell finite elements on anisotropic tetrahedral meshes

Discrete compactness, spectral correctness and verified Maxwell FEM built on the de Rham programme.

F3 · Flagship

Bound-preserving and locally conservative CR–RT methods

Combines DMP, local conservation and computable certification.

Q1 · Exact-curved geometry

Exact-curved Crouzeix–Raviart and Raviart–Thomas elements via an affine-core/curved-correction decomposition

Curved-face moments, non-affine Piola maps, divergence commutativity and equilibrated fluxes.

Q2 · Exact-curved geometry

Exact-curved shape differentiation and moving domains

Material derivatives, commutators, shape-derivative error estimates and ALE geometric conservation.

Q3 · Exact-curved geometry

Energy-stable exact-curved ALE FSI and certified interface outputs

Shared curved interfaces, work cancellation, divergence preservation and certified traction/flux outputs.
