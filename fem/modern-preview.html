Core programme

The work to build first.

The current page identifies Vision 10, 11, 17, 18, 20 and 21 as the main Core visions, with a cross-cutting certification layer.

Core G1 · Geometry / CR–RT

Anisotropic CR–RT, discrete maximum principles and maximum-norm analysis

Separate element geometry, metric-angle monotonicity, patch connectivity and discrete Green-function effects rather than forcing them into one mesh condition.

Vision 20
Core G2 · 3D de Rham

3D Nédélec interpolation to anisotropic de Rham, Hodge and Maxwell theory

Build quantitative Nédélec stability together with existing RT estimates, then move to commuting projections, discrete Poincaré inequalities, Hodge decomposition and Maxwell certification.

Vision 21
Core M · Unresolved dynamics

Stable reduction theory and structure-preserving memory computation

Vision 18 asks how history operators arise from unresolved dynamics; Vision 11 asks how a given memory or delay operator should be discretised without destroying its structure.

Visions 11 & 18
Core Q · Exact-curved geometry

Exact-curved CR–RT, shape differentiation, ALE and FSI

Keep geometry error distinct from PDE and discretisation error as exact-curved finite elements are extended to moving domains and certified interface outputs.

Vision 10
Cross-cutting V · Certification

Certified output, formal reasoning and AI4Math-ready numerical analysis

Connect equilibrated fluxes, DWR, pointwise certificates, geometric conditions and model-reduction errors into reusable and verifiable mathematical certificates.

Vision 17
