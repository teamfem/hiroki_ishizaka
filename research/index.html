Positive-type memory, graph-space well-posedness, measure-valued delays and kernel stability.

Emerging focus

Stable reduction theory for unresolved dynamics

In multiscale evolution equations, unresolved or latent variables may be removed from the resolved state without their influence disappearing. The resulting effect can survive as memory, delay, history forcing, effective dissipation or an internal-state representation.

Positive-type memoryGraph-space well-posednessMeasure-valued delayKernel stabilityOperator-valued memory
unresolved dynamics→memory representation→stable reduction→structure-preserving discretisation

The next stage is to derive memory and closure operators directly from unresolved generators, compare the energy structure of full and reduced systems, and analyse whether reduction and discretisation commute.

DG medius analysis under semi-regular geometry

  • Identify what anisotropic elements change in standard DG analysis.
  • Clarify penalty terms, condition numbers and error estimates.
  • Rebuild the older DG research note around these questions.

Helmholtz decomposition on non-simple domains

  • Multiply connected domains and domains with corners.
  • Connection with pressure robustness.
  • Influence of boundary regularity and topology.

Lagrange FEM and hypercircle certification

  • Transfer anisotropic information through RT-type flux estimates.
  • Investigate whether equilibrated fluxes bypass low-regularity interpolation restrictions in 3D.
  • Determine how computable geometric assumptions enter certified computation.
